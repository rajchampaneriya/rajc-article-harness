## The ask, then the prompts

First ask the writer for one unorganised take (3–5 minutes, stream-type, not organized first):
what happened, why now, who it should reach, what they believe, and the examples or doubts that
make the idea theirs — plus any prior writing on the topic (posts, notes, talks), treated as
source text. Because the issue body was unreadable to the agent, the take should open by
restating the idea in the writer's own words.

1. What triggered this idea this week — a conversation, a bug, a result, an annoyance? What actually happened?
2. Picture one reader. What do they already believe about this, and what should change for them after the piece?
3. Who disagrees with you on this, and what is their strongest real argument?
4. What do you say about this in conversation that you have never written down?
5. Which example from your own work carries the point? What happened, and what changed afterward — with real numbers if you are comfortable publishing them?
6. Where are you uncertain, or what have you changed your mind about?
7. What would you cut from the conventional version of this piece?
